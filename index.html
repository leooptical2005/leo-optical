About the shop

Three decades of looking after Canadians' eyes.

Leo Optical has spent the past thirty years caring for the vision of families across Canada, offering complete eye care alongside a wide selection of designer eyewear at prices built for real budgets.

As an independent shop, we're able to give the kind of personal, one-on-one attention that's hard to find at a big chain — from your first exam to picking out a frame that actually fits your face and your life.

How it works

From booking to new glasses, in four easy steps.

01

Book

Call or drop by — no online booking calendar, just a real conversation.

02

Exam

03

What we offer

Everything for your eyes, under one roof.

Comprehensive Eye Exams

A pre-testing check followed by a full exam with our licensed optometrist, who provides a prescription when needed and refers you to an ophthalmologist for any specialized care.

Insurance & Direct Billing

We accept a wide range of insurance plans and offer direct billing for eligible providers, so most of the paperwork is handled for you.

Prescription & Non-Rx Eyewear

Prescription and non-prescription glasses, fitted precisely and glazed to match your exact vision needs.

Prescription & Non-Rx Sunglasses

Sunglasses in both prescription and non-prescription options, from everyday shades to statement styles.

Prescription & Non-Rx Contacts

Fittings and supply for prescription and non-prescription contact lenses, with guidance on care and comfort.

Designer & Everyday Frames

Choose from designer name-brand or non-brand frames and sunglasses — and if the style you want isn't in stock, we'll special order it for you.

Kids' Eyewear

Durable, comfortable frames and patient, family-friendly exams for your youngest patients.

Adjustments & Repairs

Loose screw, bent frame, or a fit that's just not right anymore? Our optometrist and staff can adjust or repair it on the spot.

Lens Care & Accessories

Contact lens solutions and cases, cleaning cloths, eyewear retainers, and other vision care accessories — all in stock.
